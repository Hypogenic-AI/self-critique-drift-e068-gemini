\section{Methodology}
\label{sec:methodology}

We propose a dynamic approach to analyze the internal representational shifts during self-reflection. Our methodology focuses on \textit{representation drift}—the change in hidden state activations across reflection rounds—and its impact on the \textit{linear separability} of reasoning correctness.

\para{Experimental Conditions.} We evaluate five distinct inference conditions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0pt,topsep=0pt]
    \item \direct (Baseline): Standard zero-shot Chain-of-Thought (\CoT) generation.
    \item \rewrite (Control): Prompting the model to "rewrite the answer for clarity" without altering the reasoning. This serves as a control for "shallow" representational change.
    \item \selfone: One round of autonomous self-critique followed by a revised answer.
    \item \selfthree: Three iterative rounds of autonomous self-critique and revision.
    \item \external: Injecting task-specific "oracle" critiques (e.g., "double-check arithmetic") before asking the model to revise.
\end{enumerate}

\para{Activation Extraction.} We extract residual stream activations at the final token of the generated reasoning across multiple hidden layers (specifically at $1/4$, $1/2$, $3/4$, and the final layer of the transformer block). All extractions are performed using greedy decoding (temperature 0.0) to isolate representational changes strictly caused by the prompt condition rather than sampling variance.

\para{Metrics.} We use two primary metrics to quantify internal change:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0pt,topsep=0pt]
    \item \textbf{Representation Drift:} Calculated as the mean cosine distance between the initial draft activations and the revised activations at each layer. Drift $D = 1 - \cos(\mathbf{h}_{draft}, \mathbf{h}_{revised})$.
    \item \textbf{Linear Separability (AUC):} We train logistic regression probes on the cached activations to classify whether the final answer generated after a given condition is correct. We compare the Area Under the Curve (AUC) of the probes trained on activations before (Pre) and after (Post) the reflection process.
\end{itemize}

\para{Implementation Details.} Our experiments use \qwenonefive and \qwenzerofive from the \textsc{Qwen2.5} series. We evaluate on balanced subsets of \gsm \citep{cobbe2021gsm8k} and \csqa \citep{talmor2019csqa}. Models are run in \texttt{bfloat16} precision using the HuggingFace \texttt{transformers} library. The \gsm task uses mathematical reference answers for validation, while \csqa uses multiple-choice keys.
